Technology

Real engineering. Not a template.

Here is what happens in the seconds between a customer's message and your agent's reply.

  1. CustomerSends a message, voice note or photo on WhatsApp
  3. WhatsApp Cloud APIMeta delivers it to our secure webhook
  5. QuadNova AI engineAI model, your live catalogue and your business rules
  7. Your databaseCustomers, orders and tickets in PostgreSQL
  9. Your teamStaff alerts and the manager dashboard

Prices are never guessed

The AI never states a price on its own. Every total and stock level is checked by the server.

Private by design

Each business has its own section of the database, and WhatsApp keys are stored encrypted.

Nothing gets lost

Reminders and escalations are saved in the database, so an update or restart never drops a customer.

Data you can delete

Customers can ask for their data to be deleted, and old chats are removed automatically.

Tested on every change

Automated tests and code checks run on every update before it ships.

Runs in the cloud

Hosted in the cloud and deployed automatically, so fixes reach you fast.

How it works

Live in three steps.

We do the engineering. Your team keeps doing what it does best.

  1. We learn your business

    Your products, prices, policies and team. We load them into the system and your dashboard.

  2. We connect and configure

    Your WhatsApp number, your website, your data. Customers keep using the channels they already know.

  3. You go live and track results

    The system handles the routine work; your staff handle the rest, and you see everything on the dashboard.
